FIRE OS — Personal Financial Intelligence System

"Every rupee you spend and every minute you waste has a price — your FIRE date. Know it in real time."

What you're building

Not a budgeting app. Not a net worth tracker. A financial operating system that enforces the FIRE goal on every decision in real time.

It reads your SMS, scans receipts, parses financial documents, tracks your time, and answers one question constantly: "What does this decision cost you in days/months of FIRE?" Buy a ₹5,000 gadget and it tells you that pushed your FIRE date by 3 days. Waste 2 hours on YouTube and it converts that to the TransmuteLabs revenue you didn't generate. Set a goal, and every action is judged against it.

Five core modules
① FIRE Engine — The Goal Brain
Stores your complete financial model (exactly what we built today). Knows your corpus target, phases, income, EMIs, SIP rates. Recalculates FIRE date on every new data point. This is the single source of truth everything else reports into.
Supabase (state) Python (calc engine) FastAPI
② Ingest Layer — Read Everything
SMS reader (Android only), receipt scanner (camera), PDF/document ingestion (salary slips, bank statements, loan docs, offer letters). Every rupee in and out lands here first. Classifies automatically: income / expense / investment / EMI / one-time.
Android SMS API Claude Vision (receipts) Claude Documents (PDFs) n8n (routing)
③ Purchase Decision Engine
Before you buy anything, ask the app. Input: item + price. Output: "This costs you X days of FIRE. At your current trajectory this is Y% of this month's investable surplus. Recommended: defer / approve / avoid." Also tracks what you actually bought and pushes FIRE date accordingly.
Claude API (reasoning) FIRE Engine API React Native (UI)
④ Time → Money Converter
Tracks productive hours vs idle hours. Your hourly rate = target monthly income ÷ productive hours. Every wasted hour = a cost to the FIRE date. Integrates with calendar. If a 2-hour block has no productive tag by day-end, it's flagged. Weekly "time tax" report.
Google Calendar MCP Manual log (quick tap) Claude API (weekly analysis)
⑤ Financial Document Intelligence
Feed any document — salary slip, bank statement, Form 16, loan statement, MF statement. It extracts, classifies, and updates the FIRE model automatically. The conversation we had today? This module does that continuously, without you asking.
Claude Documents API LlamaIndex (parsing) ChromaDB (memory) n8n (pipeline)
⑥ FIRE Dashboard + Advisor
The screen you check daily. Current net worth, corpus trajectory, FIRE date (updates live), today's purchases and their FIRE cost, time efficiency score, monthly savings rate vs target. Plus a conversational advisor — ask anything, it knows your full context.

The signature interaction — "Should I buy this?"

The core UX loop that makes this different from every other app

1
You're about to buy something — a gadget, a dinner, a course. You open the app and either type it or photograph the price tag / cart screen.
2
The app looks up your current phase, this month's investable surplus remaining, and your FIRE date.
3
It tells you the real cost: "₹8,500 iPhone case. At your current ₹5.11L/mo SIP rate, this delays your FIRE date by 4.7 days. It's 1.66% of this month's surplus. You've already spent ₹42,000 on discretionary this month (82% of budget). Recommend: defer to next month."
4
You tap Approve or Defer. If approved, it logs the spend, deducts from surplus, and updates the FIRE date on the dashboard.
5
At month end, you see a "FIRE cost of lifestyle" report — total days pushed / pulled by your decisions this month.
The time-to-money mechanic

Every hour has a price. Wasted hours push FIRE.

Your target monthly income (2028)₹10L/mo
Productive hours / month (target)160 hrs
Your effective hourly rate₹6,250/hr
1 wasted evening (3 hrs)₹18,750 = 2.1 FIRE days
1 wasted weekend (16 hrs)₹1L = 11 FIRE days

The app doesn't guilt-trip about leisure. It tags time:

🟢 Productive — deep work, TransmuteLabs, client calls, coding

🟡 Maintenance — family, health, sleep, commute (cost = 0)

🔴 Drain — passive scroll, unplanned TV, untagged blocks

Only Drain time has a FIRE cost. Maintenance is free. This makes it humane, not oppressive.

The FIRE date computation (runs on every write)

Trigger: any transaction, income change, or goal update

monthly_investable =
  total_income - fixed_outflows - lifestyle

corpus_at_target_date =
  FV(current_corpus, rate, months) +
  FV_SIP(monthly_investable, rate, months)

if corpus_at_target_date >= fire_target:
  binary_search → find exact FIRE date
else:
  project forward until corpus >= target

Why this is a real product, not just a personal tool
vs. INDmoney / ET Money
They track what you have. This tells you what every decision costs in FIRE time. They're rear-view mirrors. This is a windscreen with a GPS that re-routes on every turn.
vs. YNAB / Walnut
They track budget categories. This tracks FIRE days. ₹500 dinner doesn't feel meaningful. "That dinner cost you 1.4 hours of FIRE" does. The unit of measurement is the differentiator.
vs. CFP / human advisor
A CFP updates your plan once a year. This updates it every time your salary SMS arrives, every time you scan a receipt, every time you log a decision. Always current, no appointment needed.
